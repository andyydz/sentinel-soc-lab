Code of Conduct

Our Commitment

SENTINEL SOC Lab aims to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

We welcome people with different levels of cybersecurity knowledge, technical experience, backgrounds, and perspectives.

Our goal is to encourage learning, collaboration, responsible security research, and technical improvement.

Expected Behavior

All project participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome questions and differing technical opinions.
- Provide helpful and evidence-based feedback.
- Respect other contributors' time and effort.
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them.
- Follow responsible cybersecurity research practices.

Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threatening, abusive, or intentionally hostile communication.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading contributors.
- Disrupting project discussions or collaboration.
- Encouraging unauthorized access or harmful activity.
- Sharing sensitive security information irresponsibly.

Technical Discussions

Disagreements about detection logic, investigation findings, configurations, or implementation choices are expected.

However, technical disagreements should remain respectful and focus on evidence, reproducibility, and technical reasoning rather than personal criticism.

Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for reviewing reported conduct issues and determining appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

- A warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restriction from project discussions.
- Rejection of contributions.
- Blocking participation in the project.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where participants represent or collaborate on SENTINEL SOC Lab.

Acknowledgment

Participation in SENTINEL SOC Lab means agreeing to follow these guidelines.

The objective is simple: maintain a respectful environment where cybersecurity knowledge can be shared, tested, and improved responsibly.
